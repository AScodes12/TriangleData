📋 How to Import to Scratch:

  1. Upload an image, adjust the Mesh Density slider (keeping triangles under 200,000), and click Download .txt File[cite: 3].
  2. In your Scratch project stage layout editor, right-click your empty Raw_Data list monitor and click Import[cite: 3].
  3. Select your downloaded mesh.txt file[cite: 3].
  4. Run your custom triangle filler block script loop to render the vector mesh instantly![cite: 3]
