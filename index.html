📋 How to Import to Scratch:

  1. Upload your picture, adjust the structural points slider, and click Copy Data Output.
  2. Open a text editor (like Notepad), paste the copied text values, and save it as mesh.txt.
  3. In your Scratch project layout editor workspace, right-click your empty Raw_Data list box monitor screen array and click Import.
  4. Select your mesh.txt file, then trigger your custom custom-block parsing engine loop script to render the vectors instantly!
